Sneaky Cat

Snack Heist. Humans never look up.

Noodle is a chubby tuxedo cat with a big appetite and a family that keeps the good snacks for themselves. Peek into their doll's-house home, plan a route along the counters, shelves and fridge tops, and steal the fish, the sausages and even the Sunday roast without getting caught with your paws full.

  • Humans never look up: stay above their eye line and you are invisible
  • Knock mugs and remotes off the shelves to send them the wrong way
  • Purr around their ankles to charm them for a few seconds
  • If I fits, I sits: hide in boxes and laundry baskets
  • Mum, Dad and more of the family, each with their own routine; hand-made levels that get properly hard
  • Unlock more cats, collars and hats, fill your Secret Stash, collect a daily gift
