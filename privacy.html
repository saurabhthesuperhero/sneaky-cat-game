Privacy Policy: Sneaky Cat

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Sneaky Cat ("the game", package com.saikaizen.sneakycat) is a single-player mobile game published by Saikaizen Innovations ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information is handled when you play.

Information we collect

We do not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos or any account, and the game has no sign-in, chat or other players. Your game progress (levels, stars, fish coins, cats, collars and hats, your Secret Stash and settings) is stored only on your device. We do not run our own servers for the game.

Analytics

To understand how the game is played and to fix problems (for example, which levels are too hard), the game uses Google Analytics for Firebase. It records gameplay events such as levels started and finished, hints used, fish coins earned and spent, ads watched, daily gifts claimed, shares and purchases made (the product, not your payment details), together with device information (model, Android version, language, country), an app instance identifier, the advertising identifier and approximate location derived from your IP address. This data is not linked to your name or any account and is processed by Google under the Firebase privacy terms. Detailed event data is kept for 2 months; only aggregated reports are kept longer.

Advertising

The free version of the game shows ads served by Google AdMob: banners on menu screens, occasional full-screen ads between levels, and videos you choose to watch for a reward. Ads are never shown while you are playing a level. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect and process your device's advertising identifier, approximate location (derived from your IP address), device information and how you interact with ads, and may use cookies or similar technologies. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and some US states) the game asks for your choice, through Google's consent form, before personalised ads are shown. You can change that choice at any time from Settings → Privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. Ads are limited to content rated suitable for teens.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. We never receive or store your payment details. Purchase receipts are checked on your device so that bought items (such as "Remove Ads") are granted and can be restored.

Sharing

If you choose to share the game or your Secret Stash, the game opens your device's share sheet. What you share, and with whom, is up to you; we do not receive it.

Children

The game is made for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game, contact us at the address below.

Data retention and deletion

Your progress lives only on your device: uninstalling the game or clearing its storage in Android settings deletes it. Data collected by Google for analytics and advertising is handled under Google's Privacy Policy; you can reset your advertising ID at any time. To ask us about your data, email us.

Security

Data exchanged with the analytics, advertising and billing services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
